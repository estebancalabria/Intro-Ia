### Tips

* En general las herraminetas funcionan mucho mejor con prompts en ingles
* Sirve un monton utilizar ChatGPT para que escriba los prompts mejorados por mi

```
Tengo este prompt "Haceme respetando los rasgos y expresiones faciales como un ninja de la hoja de naruto en la aldea oculta entre la niebla" dame una version mejorada para un generador de imagenes en ingles
```

* Version en ingles mejorada por IA

```
Transform me into a highly detailed ninja from the Hidden Mist Village (Kirigakure) in the Naruto universe, while faithfully preserving my original facial features, identity, facial expressions, and natural proportions.

Dress me in authentic Kirigakure shinobi attire, featuring a dark tactical outfit, a mist village forehead protector, and subtle details inspired by the Seven Ninja Swordsmen of the Mist. Set the scene in a mysterious, fog-covered village with atmospheric mist, cinematic lighting, and a dark, moody environment.

Blend my appearance naturally into the Naruto anime universe while maintaining a strong resemblance to the reference photo. Preserve my exact face shape, eyes, nose, mouth, skin tone, and distinctive features. Do not beautify, reshape, age, or alter my face.

High-quality anime-inspired realism, intricate costume details, dramatic composition, volumetric fog, sharp facial details, professional character design, cinematic atmosphere.
```
