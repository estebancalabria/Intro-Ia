## Generacion de Imagenes

* Modelos de Generacion de Imagenes
  * Open Source
    * Flux (algunas versiones)
  * Propietarios
    * Nanno Bannana
    * Gpt-Image

* Tips
  * Los prompts para generar imagenes salen mejor en ingles
  * Crear primero el prompt con ChatGPT

```
A realistic, high-quality photograph of a group of 10-year-old children celebrating a soccer victory on a football field. The children are genuinely happy, laughing, jumping, hugging each other, raising their arms, and cheering with excitement. They are wearing colorful youth soccer uniforms, with a soccer ball nearby. Capture the authentic joy, friendship, teamwork, and excitement of winning an important match. Beautiful natural daylight, green grass field, subtle stadium background, dynamic action photography, candid expressions, cinematic composition, realistic skin textures, professional sports photography, vibrant yet natural colors. Wide-angle shot, eye-level perspective, emotionally engaging, photorealistic, 4K quality.
```

## Generacion con Texto

* Hoy en dia la gran mayoria de los modelos pueden generar imagenes con Texto, pero el pionero fue uno que se llama Ideogram

* Herramienta
  * Ideogram
* URL
  * https://ideogram.ai/
* Caracteristicas
    * Ideal para publicidad y texto integrado en la imagen

```
A realistic, high-quality photograph of a group of 10-year-old children celebrating a soccer victory on a football field. The children are genuinely happy, laughing, jumping, hugging each other, raising their arms, and cheering with excitement. They are wearing colorful youth soccer uniforms, with a soccer ball nearby.

Include a large, clearly visible celebratory banner in the scene displaying the exact Spanish text: **"¡Vamos Campeones!"**. The text must be spelled exactly as written, with correct Spanish accents, highly legible, and naturally integrated into the scene. Do not translate, alter, or misspell the text.

Capture the authentic joy, friendship, teamwork, and excitement of winning an important match. Beautiful natural daylight, green grass field, subtle stadium background, dynamic action photography, candid expressions, cinematic composition, realistic skin textures, professional sports photography, vibrant yet natural colors. Wide-angle shot, eye-level perspective, emotionally engaging, photorealistic, 4K quality.
```
